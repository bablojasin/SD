## Summary
This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how fees, cancellations, and billing inquiries are managed for cybersecurity engagements with **SPECTRE DEFEND**.

---

## 1. Nature of Services & Scope of Policy
SPECTRE DEFEND provides specialized business-to-business (B2B) cybersecurity services, including managed threat detection, penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and zero-trust architecture consultations.

[spectredefend.com](https://spectredefend.com) operates as an informational and inquiry platform. Direct consumer e-commerce transactions and automated software-as-a-service self-checkout are not processed on this public website.

---

## 2. Enterprise Contracts & Bespoke Statements of Work
All commercial cybersecurity engagements, proof-of-concept deployments, and professional advisory services are governed by tailored, bilateral Master Service Agreements (MSAs) and Statements of Work (SOWs) executed between SPECTRE DEFEND and the client organization.

Refund, cancellation, credit, and milestone-acceptance terms are explicitly defined in each bilateral Statement of Work and supersede this general policy statement.

---

## 3. Project-Based Security Assessments (Penetration Testing & Audits)
For project-based engagements such as penetration testing, adversarial simulations, and architectural audits:

* **Cancellation Before Scoping Initiation:** If a client terminates an engagement in writing prior to the formal kickoff meeting or active threat reconnaissance, any unallocated advance retainer fees may be refunded, minus reasonable administrative costs incurred.
* **Cancellation After Testing Initiation:** Once active technical scoping, vulnerability probing, or red-team adversary emulation has commenced, fees corresponding to completed labor hours, infrastructure provisioning, and technical analysis are non-refundable.
* **Deliverable Acceptance:** Clients receive a formal testing report and have a specified review window (typically ten business days) to request technical clarification or milestone verification.

---

## 4. Retainer & Continuous Managed SOC Services
For ongoing managed detection and response (MDR) and 24/7 sovereign SOC surveillance retainers:

* **Subscription Billing:** Managed services are billed on a recurring monthly or annual basis as agreed in the applicable contract.
* **Termination Notice:** Contracts typically require written notice (standardly thirty to sixty days prior to renewal) to prevent automatic contract extension, as specified in your enterprise agreement.
* **Service Credits:** Where defined in a formal Service Level Agreement (SLA), service interruptions or unmet response benchmarks may be addressed through contractual service credits applied to future billing periods.
